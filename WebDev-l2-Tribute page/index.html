Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

"The Missile Man of India & People's President"

Biography

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. Despite growing up in a humble family, he worked hard and pursued his passion for science and engineering.

He joined ISRO and played an important role in developing India's first satellite launch vehicle. Later, he worked with DRDO and contributed significantly to India's missile and defense programs, earning the title "Missile Man of India."

From 2002 to 2007, Dr. Kalam served as the 11th President of India. He was widely respected for his simplicity, honesty, and dedication to education. He inspired millions of students through his speeches and books.

Even after leaving the presidency, he continued teaching and motivating young people to dream big. His life remains an inspiration for everyone who believes in hard work, knowledge, and service to the nation.

Major Achievements

  1. 1931 – Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  2. 1969 – Joined ISRO and led India's Satellite Launch Vehicle project.
  3. 1980 – Successful launch of the Rohini satellite.
  4. 1998 – Played a key role in Pokhran-II nuclear tests.
  5. 2002 – Became the 11th President of India.
  6. 1997 – Received the Bharat Ratna, India's highest civilian award.
  7. 2015 – Passed away while delivering a lecture to students.
